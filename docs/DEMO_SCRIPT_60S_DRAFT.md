## The script (~60 seconds, ~150 words)

**0:00–0:20 — Map (Section 1, "Map — real Mars background")**
"This is our Martian Map — an interactive guide to where humans could
survive on Mars. The background is real Mars imagery, not Earth streets,
and the five pins are real landing-site candidates, ranked by our Survival
Score. Oxia Planum and Utopia Planitia tie at the top with 78."

**0:20–0:40 — Compare (Section 2, "Compare two sites")**
"Compare Oxia, 78, with Jezero, 62. Jezero's 45-km crater held a lake
about 3.5 billion years ago, with a river delta that could have preserved
signs of microbial life — so the best science site is the worst survival
site. Its delta cliffs and rocks make it hard to land and live on."

**0:40–0:60 — Survival Guide (Section 3, "Survival Guide panel")**
"Pick Utopia Planitia in the guide. It has the best water on Mars — a
buried ice deposit about as much water as Lake Superior — but at 47° North
it is far colder, with much less sunlight. So water alone is not enough
to survive. That is what the score is for."

## Click cues (what to do while saying it)

1. Map: show the Mars background and the 5 pins; point to the ranking
   (Oxia and Utopia tied at 78 first, Jezero 62 last).
2. Compare: set "First site" to Oxia Planum and "Second site" to
   Jezero Crater; read the verdict.
3. Guide: set "Pick a site" to Utopia Planitia; read "If you landed
   here, you would need…" — water strength, then the cold and
   weak-sunlight warnings.
